# Big Tech AI Capex: The Research Files

These are the primary-source research files behind my Alpha with AI breakdown of what Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta are doing with their AI spending, and whether the market is mispricing it.

Every number here was pulled from primary filings and earnings calls and rebuilt with Claude Code. No Bloomberg terminal, no data subscription. Where a first pass got something wrong, a second pass against the source caught it, and the corrections are logged inside the files.

Read the full piece here: (https://ai.shikshannivesh.com/p/is-big-techs-ai-capex-mispriced-i)

## What's in here

- `capex-analysis-2025.md` : the raw capex and free cash flow numbers, the part that looks like madness at first
- `depreciation-drag.md` : why the spend turns into assets, and how depreciation reads on the income statement
- `demand-and-timing.md` : the backlog, the capacity constraint, and when these assets start paying
- `valuation.md` : where the multiples sit today, the bear read and the bull read
- `_index.md` : a map of the files above

## How to use these

Open any file to read it in your browser, or download it to drop into your own notes. If you spot something I got wrong, tell me. The whole point is that the work is checkable.
